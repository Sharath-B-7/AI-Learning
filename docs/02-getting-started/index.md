---

## Planned Category Curriculum

1. **Account Setup & Plan Selection:** Comparing Free, Pro ($20/mo), Team ($25/seat), and Enterprise capabilities.
2. **Interface Walkthrough:** Navigating the composer, sidebar, conversation history, and shortcuts.
3. **Conversation Management:** Mastering branching/forking chats, renaming, searching, and archiving.
4. **Voice & Mobile Workflows:** Using mobile dictation on iOS and Android to capture stream-of-consciousness thoughts into structured notes.
5. **Privacy & Data Safety:** Managing retention settings, data privacy boundaries, and model training opt-outs.
