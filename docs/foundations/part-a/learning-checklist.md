## Part A Competency Checklist

Use this checklist to confirm your readiness:

- [x] **[Explain what AI and LLMs are](module-01-what-is-ai.md)** in simple, plain-English words without using technical jargon (Module 1).
- [x] **[Describe how Claude produces an answer](module-03-how-llms-work.md#the-four-steps)** through pretraining, fine-tuning, and token-by-token prediction (Module 2 & 3).
- [x] **[Understand why answers vary](module-02-what-is-an-llm.md#why-answers-can-differ-each-time)** and **[why hallucinations happen](module-03-how-llms-work.md#3-hallucination)** even with a confident tone (Module 2 & 4).
- [x] **[Apply the Trust Rule](module-04-strengths-limits-trust.md#the-trust-rule-check-in-proportion-to-the-stakes)** to assess the stakes of a task and determine how thoroughly to verify the output (Module 4).
- [x] **[Follow basic privacy rules](module-04-strengths-limits-trust.md#privacy-basics)**, ensuring passwords, client PII, and unapproved secrets are never pasted into a prompt (Module 4).
- [x] **[Distinguish between a chat and an agent](module-05-meet-claude.md#chat-vs-agent)** (question-and-answer vs. autonomous multi-step execution) (Module 5).
- [x] **[Formulate requests using the Three Habits](module-05-meet-claude.md#three-habits-for-good-results):**
  1. *Context:* Who you are, the situation, and the intended audience.
  2. *Goal:* Exactly what you want created.
  3. *Format:* Word count, structure, bullet points, or tables (Module 5).
- [x] **[Complete a real everyday work task](module-05-meet-claude.md#try-it-your-first-real-work-task)** using Claude, refining the draft through multi-turn dialogue (Module 5).
- [x] **[Define key AI terms](glossary.md)** (tokens, context window, knowledge cutoff, fine-tuning, multimodal).
