> [!NOTE]
> **Who This Is For:** This part is for anyone who has finished Part A. Here we explain eight ideas that sit behind every AI tool you will use: which model to pick, what Claude can see, how to ask, how Claude keeps information between chats, how to save a good method, how Claude does real work, how Claude carries out a whole job on its own, and how you keep it safe.
> 
> The first five modules are about getting good answers in a conversation. The last three are about Claude doing work for you, and keeping that work safe.

Every idea is explained in plain English with simple everyday examples. Our examples follow **Asha**, a team lead who is learning to use Claude for everyday workplace tasks.
