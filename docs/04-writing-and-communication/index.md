> **Category Purpose:** Transform Claude into your elite writing partner, from drafting crisp emails and memos to structural proofreading and training custom writing styles.

---

## Planned Category Curriculum

1. **Drafting & Refining:** Crafting emails, memos, speeches, and proposals with clear arguments and narrative flow.
2. **Editing & Proofreading:** Line-by-line critique, tone adjustment, conciseness trimming, and grammar auditing.
3. **Using Built-in Styles:** Selecting Normal, Concise, Formal, and Explanatory presets from the composer.
4. **Creating Custom Styles:** Voice cloning—training Claude on your personal writing samples (.txt, .pdf, .docx).
5. **Creative Writing & Storytelling:** Worldbuilding, character dialogue, narrative pacing, and creative ideation.
