> [!NOTE]
> **Category Purpose:** Accelerate learning, master difficult concepts through Socratic inquiry, and leverage live web search and deep research mode for multi-source synthesis.

---

## Planned Category Curriculum

1. **Socratic Tutor Workflows:** Guiding learning step-by-step through questions rather than passive answers.
2. **Concept Breakdowns:** Explaining complex ideas across 5 levels of expertise (Child, High Schooler, Undergrad, Grad Student, Domain Expert).
3. **Live Web Search Mastery:** Finding real-time information, analyzing live web sources, and auditing inline citations.
4. **Deep Research Mode:** Compiling comprehensive, multi-source investigative dossiers on technical and scientific topics.
5. **Language Learning & Translation:** Context-aware translation, conversational roleplaying, and idiom coaching.
