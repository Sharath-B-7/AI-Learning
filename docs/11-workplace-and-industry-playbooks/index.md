> [!NOTE]
> **Category Purpose:** End-to-end practical playbooks designed for specific professional functions, providing turn-key workflows from raw input to finished business deliverables.

---

## Planned Category Curriculum

1. **Management & Operations:** 1-on-1 prep, performance reviews, OKR drafting, and executive meeting minutes.
2. **Product Management:** Turning messy notes into comprehensive PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Marketing & Social Media:** Editorial calendars, multi-platform ad variants, case studies, and SEO outlines.
4. **Sales & Customer Support:** Cold outreach sequences, objection-handling scripts, and macro ticket templates.
5. **Legal & Compliance:** NDA triage, compliance cross-checking, and contract clause comparisons.
6. **Healthcare & Life Sciences:** Medical research summarization, clinical study analysis, and patient explanations.
