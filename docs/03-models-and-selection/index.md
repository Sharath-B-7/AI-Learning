---

## Planned Category Curriculum

1. **Current Models Overview:** Deep dive on Claude 3.7 Sonnet (Hybrid Reasoning), Claude 3.5 Sonnet (v2), Claude 3.5 Haiku, and Claude 3 Opus.
2. **Model Selection Decision Matrix:** Balancing speed, reasoning depth, visual comprehension, and cost.
3. **Understanding Context Windows:** Practical limits of 200,000 tokens, needle-in-a-haystack recall, and token estimation.
4. **Legacy Models & Deprecation History:** Tracking the migration away from Claude 1 and Claude 2 series.
